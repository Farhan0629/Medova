Yoga Classes

Mindful Movement & Breath Awareness

Our general yoga classes focus on building a strong foundation through mindful movement and breath awareness. These sessions improve posture, flexibility, balance, and body awareness while helping reduce stress and mental fatigue. Designed for daily practice, they support physical health and mental clarity without strain or pressure.

Therapeutic Yoga

Healing, Recovery & Wellbeing

Therapeutic yoga sessions are thoughtfully designed to support healing, recovery, and long-term wellbeing. These classes focus on gentle movements, controlled breathing, and relaxation techniques that help ease stiffness, reduce discomfort, and improve movement. Each session is adapted to individual needs, making it ideal for stress management, recovery support, and lifestyle-related concerns.
