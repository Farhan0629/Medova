Beginner-Friendly Healthy Diet Plan

A healthy diet doesn't need to be complicated or restrictive. For beginners, the focus should be on creating balanced meals that provide steady energy, support overall health, and are easy to maintain in daily life.

This beginner-friendly diet plan focuses on simple food choices, balanced nutrition, and regular eating patterns. It aims to help individuals improve energy levels, maintain a healthy metabolism, and support long-term wellness without extreme dieting.

This plan is suitable for individuals who are starting their health journey and want a sustainable way to improve their eating habits.
