10 Simple Daily Habits for a Healthy Life

Home / Blog / 10 Simple Daily Habits for a Healthy Life

By Ravi Kishore · Dec 22, 2025

Health is defined by the World Health Organization (WHO) as a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being; not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.

Many health issues can be addressed holistically through lifestyle modifications.

1. Drinking enough water

Staying hydrated is key. Drinking enough water helps your body function properly, boosts energy, and even supports weight loss. Aim for at least 8-10 glasses a day, but it can vary depending on activity level and climate. Listen to your body and drink when you're thirsty.

2. Eat balanced meals

A healthy diet comprises a combination of different foods. These include:

  • Staples like cereals (wheat, barley, rye, maize or rice) or starchy tubers or roots (potato, yam, taro or cassava).
  • Legumes (lentils and beans).
  • Fruit and vegetables.
  • Foods from animal sources (meat, fish, eggs and milk).

3. Exercise regularly

Regular physical activity can reduce the risk of heart disease. It can improve cardiovascular fitness, decrease blood pressure and in losing and maintaining weight and alter blood lipid and lipoprotein levels. Brisk walking for at least 40 minutes several times a week significantly reduces the risk of heart failure.

4. Get proper sleep

Getting 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night can boost mood, improve concentration, and even support weight management. Establish a bedtime routine, avoid screens before bed, and create a good sleep environment.

5. Reduce stress

Physical activity reduces stress. Research shows that people who work out even once a week or for as little as 10 minutes a day tend to be more cheerful. Yoga and breathing exercises bring joy. Meditation is a powerful tool to integrate all six components of health.

6. Limit sugar and salt intake

Restrict total salt intake to less than 5 grams per day (about 1 teaspoon).

Sources to Avoid: Minimize processed and preserved foods, including pickles, papads, chutneys, ketchup, sauces, bakery items, biscuits, chips, cheese, and salted fish.

Keep added sugar intake below 5% of your total daily energy intake.

Reduce consumption of sugary drinks, cold drinks, packaged fruit juices, sweets, cakes, and cookies.

7. Maintain personal hygiene

Personal hygiene refers to regularly washing parts of the body and hair with soap and water (including washing your hands and feet regularly), grooming nails, facial cleanliness, covering coughs and sneezes, and menstrual hygiene. Personal hygiene practices can help you to feel fresh and healthy.

8. Avoid smoking and alcohol

Smoking and alcohol adversely affect immunity and increase the risk and severity of infections, hence must be avoided.

9. Regular health check-ups

Regular health check-ups can identify any early signs of health issues. For a healthy lifestyle, typically, docs recommend:

  • BP and sugar checks annually
  • Cholesterol checks every 5 years
  • Cancer screenings (like mammograms, Pap smears) as age demands
  • Dental and eye checks yearly

10. Stay positive

Positive thinking helps with stress management and can even improve your health.

A Healthy lifestyle is a holistic long term, commitment to physical, mental and social well being rather than a quick fix. By combining a nutritious diet, regular exercise, adequate sleep, and stress management, you can significantly prevent diseases, increase energy, and improve longevity. Ultimately, it is a personal and conscious choice to foster a balanced life.
