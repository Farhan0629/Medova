Hydration Guidelines

Proper hydration is essential for maintaining energy and supporting metabolism.

Recommended hydration practices:

  • Drink 6–8 glasses of water daily
  • Include hydrating drinks such as:
  • Coconut water
  • Herbal tea
  • Lemon water
  • Buttermilk

💡 Reminder

Limit sugary beverages and excessive caffeine.
